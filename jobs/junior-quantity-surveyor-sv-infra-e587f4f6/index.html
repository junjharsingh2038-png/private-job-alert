Junior Quantity Surveyor

SV Infra

📍 Hyderbad   🎓 B.Tech/B.E. in Civil Engineering.   💰 50000   🕒 Full Time

Job Description

We are seeking an experienced Quantity Surveyor with site QA/QC exposure to join our on-site construction team. You will be responsible for cost estimation, bill preparation, subcontractor billing, and material reconciliation, while also conducting basic quality inspections to ensure all civil works align with project specifications. Key Responsibilities • Quantity Surveying & Billing: o Prepare and verify Bill of Quantities (BOQ), bar bending schedules (BBS), and rate analysis. o Certify subcontractor running account (RA) bills and track variation claims. o Conduct material reconciliation (steel, cement, aggregates, finishing materials) to prevent wastage. o Assist in budget forecasting and overall site cost control. • Quality Check (QA/QC): o Perform routine site quality inspections across concrete pours, reinforcement, blockwork, and plastering. o Review incoming raw material test certificates and cross-check site test results (cube tests, slump tests, etc.). o Ensure civil execution adheres strictly to architectural drawings, structural details, and quality standards. o Identify site defects early and coordinate with site engineers to issue corrective actions.
