Store incharge

Rean Watertech Pvt. Ltd

📍 Rajasthan / Jaipur /Ajmer/ near bay   🎓 Graduate   💰 As per interview   🕒 Full Time

Job Description

Store In-Charge – Job Responsibilities - Manage day-to-day store and warehouse operations. - Receive, inspect, and verify materials against purchase orders and delivery documents. - Maintain accurate inventory records in ERP/Excel and ensure physical stock accuracy. - Handle material receipt, storage, issue, transfer, and return processes. - Monitor stock levels and coordinate for timely replenishment of materials. - Conduct regular physical stock verification and resolve stock discrepancies. - Maintain proper documentation including GRN, Material Issue Notes, Stock Registers, and Return Records. - Ensure proper identification, labeling, stacking, and safe storage of materials. - Monitor slow-moving, damaged, excess, and obsolete stock and report to management. - Coordinate with Purchase, Accounts, Production, Project, and other departments for material requirements. - Prepare daily, weekly, and monthly stock and inventory reports. - Ensure store housekeeping, safety, and compliance with company procedures. - Supervise store staff and allocate daily work responsibilities. - Support internal and external stock audits and ensure proper documentation.
