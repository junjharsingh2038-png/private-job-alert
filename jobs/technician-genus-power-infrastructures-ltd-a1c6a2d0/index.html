Technician

Genus Power Infrastructures Ltd

📍 Barmer   🎓 ITI / Any Diploma   💰 15000   🕒 Full Time

Job Description

• Safely remove old electricity meters and install single-phase and three-phase smart meters.

• Check wiring connections, earthing, and load before and after installation.

• Test meter power, display, and network/communication connectivity on-site.

• Update meter serial numbers, old/new readings, and installation photos in the mobile app.

• Troubleshoot basic wiring, display, or network issues and handle meter replacements.

• Follow all electrical safety rules, wear proper PPE, and coordinate politely with consumers.

Requirements & Qualifications:

• Education: ITI (Electrician / Wireman) or Diploma in Electrical Engineering (10th/12th pass with relevant experience may also apply).

• Experience: 0–2+ years in electrical installation, meter reading, or smart meter projects (freshers with ITI can apply).

• Skills: Basic knowledge of single-phase/three-phase wiring, smartphone usage for field reporting apps, and good communication.

• Other: Valid two-wheeler driving license and own bike preferred for field travel.

Benefits:

• Competitive monthly salary + per-meter installation incentives

• Petrol / travel allowance

• Safety kit and basic tools provided

• PF & ESIC benefits (as applicable)
