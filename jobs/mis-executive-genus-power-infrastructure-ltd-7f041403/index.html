MIS Executive – Job Description Job Title: MIS Executive Department: MIS / Operations Location: As per company requirement Reporting To: MIS Manager / Operations Manager Job Summary: MIS Executive is responsible for collecting, maintaining, analyzing and reporting business data. The role requires strong knowledge of MS Excel, data management, reporting and dashboard preparation to provide accurate and timely information to management. Key Responsibilities: Prepare Daily, Weekly and Monthly MIS reports. Collect data from different departments/teams and consolidate it. Maintain accurate and updated databases and records. Prepare performance, productivity, attendance and target vs. achievement reports. Create and maintain Excel dashboards and management reports. Verify and reconcile data to ensure accuracy. Track pending activities, exceptions and deviations and highlight them to management. Prepare ad-hoc reports as required by management. Use Pivot Tables, VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP, SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, charts and Excel formulas for data analysis. Identify trends and provide useful insights from data. Maintain proper backup and documentation of MIS reports. Coordinate with different departments for timely submission of data. Improve and automate existing reporting processes wherever possible. Ensure all reports are submitted within the defined TAT (Turnaround Time). Required Skills: Excellent knowledge of MS Excel. Good analytical and problem-solving skills. Good knowledge of data handling and reporting. Basic knowledge of Power BI will be an advantage. Good communication and coordination skills. High attention to detail and accuracy. Ability to work under deadlines. Qualification & Experience: Graduate in any discipline; preference may be given to candidates with B.Com/BBA/BCA/MBA. 1–3 years of relevant MIS/reporting experience preferred. Key Performance Indicators (KPI): Report accuracy Timely submission of MIS Data accuracy and reconciliation Dashboard/report quality Ad-hoc report turnaround time Data availability and completeness Process automation and improvement
