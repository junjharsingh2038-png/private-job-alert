Site account

Rean Watertech Pvt. Ltd

📍 Rajasthan / jaipur / Ajmer / Nearby   🎓 Graduate   💰 As per interview   🕒 Full Time

Job Description

Site Accountant – Job Responsibilities Handle day-to-day accounting activities at the project/site office. Maintain cash book, petty cash, vouchers, bills, and supporting documents. Record daily expenses and ensure proper authorization of payments. Verify vendor bills, invoices, purchase bills, and supporting documents. Maintain site-wise expenses, advances, and employee/vendor payment records. Prepare and maintain daily, weekly, and monthly financial reports. Handle petty cash and maintain proper cash reconciliation. Coordinate with the Head Office for payments, accounting entries, and financial requirements. Maintain records of site purchases, material expenses, labour payments, and other project-related costs. Track employee and vendor advances and ensure timely settlement. Assist in GST/TDS-related documentation and compliance as required. Reconcile cash, bank, vendor, and other accounts regularly. Maintain proper filing of invoices, vouchers, receipts, and accounting documents. Support internal and external audits by providing required records and documents. Ensure compliance with company accounting policies and procedures.
